肖像与身体Portrait and Body

肖像摄影从长时间曝光开始，被拍者必须保持不动；后来快门变快，表情和姿态才成为可捕捉的东西。肖像从来不只是“像不像”，而是谁有权决定一个人的样子。Portrait photography began with long exposures that demanded stillness; faster shutters later made expression and gesture capturable. A portrait is never only likeness but who has the power to decide how a person looks.
