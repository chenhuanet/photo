建筑与空间Architecture and Space

建筑摄影不只是拍立面，而是决定视点、透视、光线和人的尺度。一张建筑照片可以让你觉得自己站在门口，也可以把整座城市压成一张明信片。Architectural photography is not only façades but viewpoint, perspective, light and human scale. A photograph can place you at the door or press a whole city into a postcard.
