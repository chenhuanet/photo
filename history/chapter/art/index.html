章节CHAPTER

艺术与图像Art and Image

画意摄影模仿绘画，直接摄影拒绝模仿，观念摄影则把制度、复制和观看本身变成题材。摄影进入美术馆的同时，也不断揭穿美术馆的规则。Pictorialism imitated painting, straight photography refused imitation, and conceptual photography made institutions, reproduction and looking itself the subject. Photography entered the museum while exposing the museum's rules.

摄影如何成为艺术，又为何不断怀疑艺术？How does photography become art, and why does it keep doubting art?
