城市与街道City and Street

城市摄影不只是拍建筑，而是拍人如何相遇、避让、交易和等待。街道是公开的，也是被权力组织过的；镜头选择谁进入画面，谁被裁掉。Urban photography is not only architecture but how people meet, avoid, trade and wait. The street is public and organised by power; the lens chooses who enters the frame and who is cropped out.
