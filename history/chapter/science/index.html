科学与太空Science and Space

天文、医学、显微镜和航天摄影把肉眼看不到的尺度变成图像。这些图像不是“自然的样子”，而是仪器、数据和解释共同构造的结果。Astronomy, medicine, microscopy and space photography turn scales invisible to the eye into images. These images are not 'how nature looks' but the result of instruments, data and interpretation working together.
