帝国与殖民Empire and Colonialism

远征摄影把遗址、道路、人群和“类型”拍成可运输的知识。照片既记录了被殖民地的样子，也参与了分类、命名和占有。Expedition photography turned ruins, roads, crowds and 'types' into transportable knowledge. The photographs recorded colonised places and took part in classifying, naming and possessing them.
