旅游与休闲Travel and Leisure

旅游摄影把地方压缩成几个可识别的符号：塔、桥、海滩、日落。它让远方变得可达，也让远方变成重复的图像。Travel photography compresses place into a few recognisable signs: tower, bridge, beach, sunset. It makes distance reachable and turns distance into a repeated image.
