风景与领土Landscape and Territory

风景摄影从“如画”的传统出发，却常被测绘、铁路、旅游和环保运动征用。山、河、森林不是空的，它们被边界、所有权和叙述同时占有。Landscape photography begins with the picturesque but is often enlisted by survey, railway, tourism and environmental movements. Mountain, river and forest are not empty; they are possessed by borders, property and narrative at once.
