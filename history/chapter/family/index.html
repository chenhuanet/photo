家庭与童年Family and Childhood

家庭摄影是私人记忆，也是社会规范。谁站在中间、谁抱着孩子、谁不在画面里，说明的不只是亲情，还有性别、阶层和代际权力。Family photography is private memory and social norm. Who stands at the centre, who holds the child, who is absent speaks not only of affection but of gender, class and generational power.
