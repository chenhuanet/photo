冷战、彩色与消费Cold War, Colour and Consumption

彩色胶片、杂志、广告和太空计划让摄影同时服务于消费幻想和国家竞赛。照片既是家庭记忆，也是意识形态的证明材料。Colour film, magazines, advertising and the space programme made photography serve both consumer fantasy and national rivalry. The photograph was family memory and ideological evidence at once.
