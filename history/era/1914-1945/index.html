时代ERA

战争与媒体War and the Media

两次世界大战把相机变成前线装备和国家叙事工具。新闻、宣传、航拍和档案摄影同时扩张，照片成为证明、动员和审查的对象。Two world wars turned the camera into frontline equipment and a tool of national narrative. News, propaganda, aerial survey and archive photography expanded together; photographs became objects of proof, mobilisation and censorship.
