时代ERA

技术的诞生The Birth of Technique

银版、盐纸、蛋白印相和第一批商业工作室，让摄影从实验室走向街道。曝光时间从分钟降到秒，肖像第一次成为普通人可以买到的图像。Daguerreotype, salt print, albumen and the first commercial studios took photography from the laboratory to the street. Exposure fell from minutes to seconds, and the portrait became an image ordinary people could buy.
