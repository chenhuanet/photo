五个时代，八章，一部从化学到卫星的影像史Five eras, eight chapters: an image history from chemistry to satellites

这是“影像档案版”：以 1734 张有年代、有出处的摄影记录为主轴，380 篇故事为叙事；不是只拿故事配图拼成的“图说史”。This is an archive edition: 1734 dated, sourced photographs form the spine, with 380 stories as narrative. It is not a picture-book assembled only from story illustrations.

口径：档案照片按记录的对象年代归档，不等于“拍摄于该年”的绝对证据；故事按事件年份编排。两类材料都保留出处，不用配图冒充现场。Method: archive photographs are grouped by the object date in the record, not treated as absolute proof that the shutter was pressed that year; stories are ordered by event year. Both keep their sources; illustrations do not stand in for evidence.
