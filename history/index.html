摄影史History of Photography

七段时代、十六章、六十位摄影家：一部从化学到卫星的影像史Seven eras, sixteen chapters, sixty photographers: an image history from chemistry to satellites

这一版把摄影史做成可走进去的档案：时代给尺度，章节给问题，摄影家给手，工艺给材料，主题与展览给横向路线。每一页都从本馆藏的真实记录出发，不拿无关图片填空。This edition makes photographic history a walkable archive: eras give scale, chapters give questions, photographers give hands, processes give material, themes and exhibitions give lateral routes. Every page starts from real records in this archive and does not fill gaps with unrelated pictures.
