银版摄影Daguerreotype

银版是第一种公开的商业摄影法：铜板镀银、碘化、曝光、汞显影。每张都是唯一正像，细节极细，但无法复制，曝光也长。The daguerreotype was the first publicly commercial photographic process: silvered copper, iodine, exposure, mercury development. Each plate is a unique positive with extreme detail, but it cannot be reproduced and exposure is long.
