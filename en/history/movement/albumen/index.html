流派MOVEMENT

蛋白印相Albumen Print

蛋白印相用蛋清涂层纸，表面光滑、色调温暖，适合大尺寸和商业发行。19世纪下半叶的旅行、遗址和肖像大多用它。Albumen prints coat paper with egg white, giving a smooth surface and warm tone suited to large formats and commercial distribution. Most later nineteenth-century travel, ruin and portrait photographs use it.
