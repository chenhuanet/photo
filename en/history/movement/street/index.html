街头摄影Street Photography

街头摄影在公共空间里等待偶然：一个眼神、一次错身、一块广告牌和一个人。它依赖快门的时机，也依赖摄影师的停留。Street photography waits for accident in public space: a glance, a passing, a billboard and a person. It depends on shutter timing and on the photographer's lingering.
