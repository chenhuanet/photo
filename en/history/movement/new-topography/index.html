流派MOVEMENT

新地形摄影New Topography

新地形摄影以冷静、平视的方式拍郊区、公路、停车场和工业边缘。它不把风景浪漫化，而把人类改造过的土地当作新的自然。New Topography photographs suburb, highway, parking lot and industrial edge in a cool, level way. It does not romanticise landscape but treats human-modified land as the new nature.
