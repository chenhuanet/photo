流派MOVEMENT

直接摄影Straight Photography

直接摄影拒绝柔焦和画意修饰，强调清晰、完整和照相机自身的观看。它把“像照片”变成一种美学。Straight photography rejects soft focus and pictorial retouching, insisting on sharpness, completeness and the camera's own way of seeing. It makes 'looking like a photograph' an aesthetic.
