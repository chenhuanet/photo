银版法Daguerreotype

铜板镀银、碘化、曝光、汞显影，得到唯一正像。细节极细，但每张都不可复制。Silvered copper, iodine, exposure and mercury development produce a unique positive. Detail is extreme, but each plate cannot be reproduced.
