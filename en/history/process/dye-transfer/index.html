工艺PROCESS

染料转印Dye Transfer

染料转印用三层染料分别转印，色彩控制极强，是高端商业和艺术复制的手段。它昂贵、缓慢，却极其稳定。Dye transfer prints three dye layers separately, giving exceptional colour control for high-end commercial and fine-art reproduction. It is expensive, slow and very stable.
