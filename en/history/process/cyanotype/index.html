蓝晒Cyanotype

铁盐感光，水洗后得到普鲁士蓝图像。成本低、便于复制，常用于植物标本、图纸和业余摄影。Iron salts produce a Prussian-blue image after washing. Cheap and easy to reproduce, it was used for botanical specimens, plans and amateur photography.
