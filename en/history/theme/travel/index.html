旅游Travel

旅游摄影把地方压缩成可识别的符号：塔、桥、海滩、日落。它让远方可达，也让远方重复。Travel photography compresses place into recognisable signs: tower, bridge, beach, sunset. It makes distance reachable and repetitive.
