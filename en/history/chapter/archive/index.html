档案与记忆Archive and Memory

档案不是中立仓库。它由机构、国家、家庭和商业选择构成；被保存的照片塑造后世的历史感，被丢弃的照片则沉默。The archive is not a neutral store. It is shaped by institutions, states, families and commerce; what is kept shapes later history, what is discarded stays silent.
