技术的诞生The Birth of Technique

摄影首先是一项技术：感光材料、镜头、快门和暗房。但技术从不中性：曝光时间决定谁能被拍，底片尺寸决定什么能被放大，印刷方式决定图像能走多远。Photography is first a technology: sensitive material, lens, shutter and darkroom. But technology is never neutral: exposure decides who can be photographed, negative size decides what can be enlarged, printing decides how far an image can travel.
