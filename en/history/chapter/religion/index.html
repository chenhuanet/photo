宗教与仪式Religion and Ritual

宗教摄影拍建筑、仪式、朝圣和身体动作，但信仰本身不可见。照片只能拍它的物质痕迹：烛光、经书、队列、手势和空间。Religious photography shows buildings, rituals, pilgrimage and bodily action, but faith itself is invisible. The photograph can only show its material traces: candlelight, scripture, procession, gesture and space.
