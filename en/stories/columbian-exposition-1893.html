1893: Chicago's White City

1 May: the World's Columbian Exposition opens · Chicago, Illinois

Opening

On 1 May 1893 the World's Columbian Exposition opened in Chicago, marking four hundred years since Columbus reached the Americas. It ran until October, showing industry, transport, architecture and exhibits from around the world.

1893 年，芝加哥世博会交通馆的入口与门廊。
交通馆的“金色门廊”。

The White City

The main buildings were grouped in the “White City”, built largely of white stucco with classical columns and lit by electric light at night. It presented an idealised image of a city, along with the novelty of electricity and machinery.

世博会的赌场建筑。
新英格兰木屋内的一个房间。
“海难”展区。

Midway and human display

The fair's Midway showed villages and peoples from different regions. Samoans, Alaska Natives and others were placed in enclosures for visitors to watch. This was part of the colonial-era “human display” and is a side of the fair that cannot be ignored.
