Visual dossier · 1935-09-30

1935: Hoover Dam completed

30 September: the Colorado River is stopped · Boulder Canyon, USA

The site in the canyon

In 1931 a consortium of six companies began work in Black Canyon. During the Depression thousands of workers came to Nevada and settled in the new town of Boulder City. The workforce included Native American workers, Black labourers and white skilled workers, separated by race in jobs and housing. Official records list 96 worker deaths during construction.

运送工人往返大坝工地的双层通勤车。
胡佛水坝工地上的原住民工人。
施工期间的一名原住民劳工。

Pouring

The dam is a concrete arch, about 221 metres high, built from some 3.25 million cubic yards of concrete poured in blocks, with cooling pipes carrying away the heat inside to prevent cracking. In 1933 the first bucket of concrete went into the foundation.

第一斗八立方码混凝土浇筑。
内华达溢洪道的面板施工。
1934 年，施工中的进水塔。

Dedication

On 30 September 1935 President Roosevelt spoke at the dedication. The reservoir, Lake Mead, was then among the largest artificial lakes in the world. The dam was completed in March 1936 and began to provide flood control, irrigation and power.

1935 年 5 月，从亚利桑那一侧下游看胡佛水坝。
1935 年 5 月，胡佛水坝上游面。

Afterward

Hoover Dam controlled floods on the lower Colorado and supplied water and electricity to Los Angeles, Las Vegas and surrounding farmland. At the same time, the reservoir drowned canyons and Native American land, and less water reached the sea; the ecological cost continues.

1934 年，亚利桑那一侧的进水塔与溢洪道。
1935 年，米德湖与进水塔。
