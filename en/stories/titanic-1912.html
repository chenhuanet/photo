Visual dossier · 1912-04-10

Titanic: leaving Southampton

10 April 1912: an “unsinkable” ship begins her first voyage · Southampton, England

Departure

On 10 April 1912 Titanic sailed from Southampton, called at Cherbourg in France and Queenstown in Ireland, then crossed the Atlantic. She was the largest passenger liner in the world and the pride of the White Star Line.

1912 年 4 月 10 日，泰坦尼克号驶离南安普敦。

The iceberg

Late on 14 April Titanic struck an iceberg in the North Atlantic. Her hull was opened in several places on the starboard side, and she sank in the early hours of 15 April. Arguments later arose over her design, lookouts, lifeboats and radio; what is certain is that there were not enough lifeboats for everyone aboard.

Rescue

Carpathia received the distress call and came from some distance, rescuing about seven hundred survivors. They were taken to New York; hospitals, lifeboats and life jackets became the physical evidence of the disaster.

泰坦尼克号幸存者在卡帕西亚号上。
1912 年，纽约圣文森特医院的泰坦尼克号幸存者。

Afterward

After the disaster, maritime safety rules, lifeboat standards and radio watch requirements changed. Titanic never completed her first voyage, but her name remained in the memory of twentieth-century shipwrecks.
