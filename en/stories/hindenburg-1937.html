1937: the last minute of the Hindenburg

6 May, Lakehurst · Lakehurst, New Jersey

Arrival

In the evening of 6 May 1937, the Hindenburg arrived at the naval air station at Lakehurst, New Jersey. It had just completed a passenger crossing of the Atlantic and was preparing to moor. Ground crew and reporters were waiting for it to land.

1937 年 5 月 6 日，兴登堡号在莱克赫斯特起火。
兴登堡号灾难现场。

Fire

At about 7:25 p.m. fire broke out at the tail of the gas cells and engulfed the airship within seconds. There were 97 people aboard; including one ground crew member, 36 died. A radio reporter's live account became one of the earliest disaster broadcasts.

燃烧中的兴登堡号残骸。
兴登堡号起火。
1937 年，兴登堡号燃烧。
火焰中的兴登堡号残骸。

Wreck

When the fire died down, only the metal frame and burned skin remained. Investigations proposed several possible causes, including static electricity, leaking hydrogen and a flammable outer coating; the argument continues.

兴登堡号残骸。
兴登堡号残骸近景。
1937 年，兴登堡号灾难后的残骸。

Afterward

The Hindenburg disaster was recorded on film and radio and became one of the most famous disaster images of the twentieth century. It also brought the era of large passenger airships to a rapid end, and fixed-wing aircraft became the main means of crossing the Atlantic.
