Visual dossier · 1974-11-16

A letter to the universe

16 November 1974: 1,679 bits sent toward M13

Leaving the surface

In 1957 Sputnik put the first artificial satellite in orbit; in 1961 Gagarin and Shepard entered space; in 1965 White opened the hatch of Gemini 4. For the first time humans were no longer only looking up from the ground — they had begun to see themselves from outside.

1957 年，斯普特尼克。第一颗人造地球卫星升空，太空时代开始。
1961 年，艾伦·谢泼德在自由 7 号舱内。美国第一次载人亚轨道飞行。
1965 年，爱德华·怀特完成美国第一次太空行走。人第一次在舱外漂浮。

Seeing Earth for the first time

In 1968 Apollo 8 photographed “Earthrise” while orbiting the Moon; in 1969 Apollo 11 put people on the lunar surface; in 1972 Apollo 17 took the full “Blue Marble”. Earth had become a portrait that could be framed.

1968 年，阿波罗 8 号拍摄的“地出”。地球从月面边缘升起。
1969 年，阿波罗 11 号的奥尔德林在月面。人类第一次站到另一个天体上。
1972 年，阿波罗 17 号拍摄的“蓝色弹珠”。一张完整的地球肖像。

1974: Earth written as a telegram

On 16 November 1974, the Arecibo telescope in Puerto Rico sent a 1,679-bit radio message toward the globular cluster M13. In binary it described numbers, the elemental makeup of DNA, the human figure, the solar system and the telescope itself. At the speed of light, the message needs about 20,000 years to arrive. It was more a technical demonstration than a true interstellar dialogue. This archive holds no image of Arecibo itself; this chapter uses photographs of Earth from space taken in the same period to set the scene.

1973 年，Skylab 拍摄的太阳耀斑。人类开始在太空长期观测太阳。
1974 年，Skylab 拍摄的地球边缘。同年 11 月 16 日，阿雷西博把信息发向 M13。

After the message

In 1977 Voyager 1 and 2 lifted off carrying the Golden Record: greetings, music and the sounds of Earth. Arecibo's message was sent once; the Voyager records are travelling farther. No one knows whether anyone will receive them, but both wrote “we are here” into the universe.

1977 年，旅行者号镀金唱片。人类把问候、音乐和地球的声音送向星际空间。
