Visual dossier · 1889-03-31

1889: the tower is topped out

31 March: Paris gains a 300-metre tower · Paris, France

Controversy and foundations

In 1887 Paris began building a 300-metre tower for the 1889 world's fair. The design came from Gustave Eiffel's engineering firm. More than three hundred writers, painters and architects protested that it would ruin the Paris skyline. Work went ahead as planned, with the foundations driven into the soil beside the Seine.

1888 年 2 月，铁塔的地基与底层结构。
工地上的德科维尔轻便铁路，用来运送铁件。

Floor by floor

The tower was made of more than eighteen thousand iron parts and about 2.5 million rivets. Without large cranes, workers hoisted the pieces one by one. Four inclined legs converged upward and were joined at each platform. Photographs record it growing taller through 1888.

1888 年 4 月 10 日的铁塔。

Topping out and opening

On 31 March 1889 the main structure was finished. In May the world's fair opened and the tower was opened to the public. It was originally meant to stand for only twenty years; the radio antenna at the top gave it military and communications value, and it was kept.
