一、关于本站1. About this site

尘画（出尘入画）是一个非商业性、开放的历史影像索引与展示项目。本站不设账号、不投放广告、不出售图片，不以影像本身直接营利。

Frame & Grain (Out of dust, into the image) is a non-commercial, open index and display of historical images. The site has no accounts, no advertising and does not sell images; it does not profit directly from the images themselves.

站内影像按主题、年代与日期整理，逐条标注来源、作者与许可；整理工作只做索引与展示，不代替任何馆藏机构或权利人的官方说明。

Images are organised by theme, period and date, with source, creator and licence noted record by record. The work is an index and display only; it does not replace any institution's or rights holder's official description.

尘归尘，画归画：影像来自历史，也终将回到历史；本站只做整理、索引与展示，不主张对历史影像本身的著作权。

Dust to dust, image to image: images come from history and return to it. The site only organises, indexes and displays them, and claims no copyright in the historical images themselves.
