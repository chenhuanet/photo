织机前的男孩The boy at the loom

美国 印第安纳Indiana, United States·纪实照片Documentary photograph可自由商用照片Photograph
印第安纳州棉纺厂，1908 年前后。他站在织机前，身高刚好够得着机器。历史书里四个字的「工业革命」，落到一个人身上就是这个高度。尘画把这张和登月放在同一条时间轴上，不是抬举它，是提醒自己别把尺度搞反。

A cotton mill in Indiana, around 1908. He stands at the loom, just tall enough to reach the machine. The four characters “Industrial Revolution” land on one person at exactly this height. Frame & Grain puts this on the same timeline as the Moon landing not to elevate it, but to keep the scale from flipping.
