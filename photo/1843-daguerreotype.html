一个人把自己拍进了画室A man photographed himself into the studio

法国 / 欧洲France / Europe·银版Daguerreotype可自由商用照片Photograph
1839 年达盖尔公布银版法，没几年就有人把镜头掉转过来对准自己。此前要留下「我在这里」，得靠画笔和别人的手；此后，光自己就能办到。尘画要收的，正是从这一刻开始的所有画面——包括这一帧略显紧张的自拍。

In 1839 Daguerre announced the daguerreotype. Within a few years someone had turned the lens on himself. Before that, leaving a likeness of “I am here” took a brush and someone else's hand; after, light could do it alone. What Frame & Grain collects begins at this moment — including this slightly nervous self-portrait.
