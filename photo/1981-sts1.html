STS-1 升空STS-1 lifts off

美国 肯尼迪航天中心Kennedy Space Center, United States·航天影像Space photographyU1照片Photograph
NASA 的原始说明开头是「六年的沉默之后」。1981 年 4 月 12 日，航天飞机第一次离开发射台。连官方档案都忍不住用一句带情绪的话起头，可见等待有多长。

NASA's original caption begins, “After six years of silence.” On 12 April 1981 the shuttle left the launch pad for the first time. Even the official archive could not resist an emotional opening — a measure of how long the wait had been.
