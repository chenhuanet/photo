躺着的士兵The soldier lying down

美国United States·官方档案Official archive可自由商用照片Photograph
美国内战期间的一张肖像，人是躺着的。没有冲锋，没有旗帜，只有一个姿势。大事件从来不只由将领和日期构成，它最后总要落到一个具体的人身上——这里，连他叫什么都没留下。

A portrait from the American Civil War, and the man is lying down. No charge, no flag, only a pose. Great events are never made only of generals and dates; in the end they land on one specific person — here, not even his name survives.
